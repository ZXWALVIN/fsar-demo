Patent CN202311836940.X | Micro-Simulation v2.3-L | 2026-03-06

Abstract

This study presents FSAR (Fluid Segregation & Active Relief), a novel traffic engineering approach that eliminates turbulent flow at highway merge points through topological inversion and vacuum isolation. Comparative micro-simulation demonstrates a 21% increase in downstream throughput, 17% improvement in average speed, and 98.8% reduction in stop-and-go events compared to conventional parallel acceleration lanes.
